Everything in one place

Built for the way landlords actually work.

From the first tenant to the final settlement — track it all without spreadsheets or paper receipts.

Properties & rooms

Manage PGs, apartments and hostels. Split each into rooms with rent, capacity and status.

Tenant records

Full profiles, ID proofs, deposits and rent share — with a soft move-out that keeps history safe.

Auto rent reminders

A smart engine emails, SMSes and WhatsApps tenants the day rent is due — with your UPI details.

Payments & UPI

Record rent, store your UPI ID & QR, and push each tenant's next-due date forward automatically.

Dashboard insights

Occupancy, rent collected this month and pending dues — the numbers that matter, at a glance.

Tenant credit score

Every tenant carries a signed reliability score, built from how they actually pay — so you always know who pays on time.
